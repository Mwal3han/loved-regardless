ABOUT ME

The person behind Loved; Regardless.

HI, I'M HANNAH.

Welcome to Loved; Regardless.

This website is a little collection of my thoughts, experiences, interests, and the things that make me who I am.

I'm figuring life out one season at a time. I'm learning, growing, making mistakes, starting over, and trying to enjoy the process along the way.

I created this space because I wanted somewhere that felt like mine. Somewhere I could write freely, share pieces of my life, talk about music, and document the person I'm becoming.

WHY "LOVED; REGARDLESS"?

Because I believe love shouldn't disappear just because life isn't perfect.

Loved when I'm succeeding. Loved when I'm struggling. Loved when I'm confident. Loved when I'm still figuring things out.

That's the heart behind this website.
