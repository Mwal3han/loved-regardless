When I Realized My Passion Can't Afford My Cost of Living

Sometimes the thing you love and the life you need don't immediately look like they belong together.

There comes a point where you have to be honest with yourself about what you want, what you need, and what your current reality actually looks like.

For a long time, I thought having a passion meant I automatically had to build my entire life around it. But I'm learning that passion and practicality don't always have to compete.

WHEN PASSION MEETS REALITY

Loving something doesn't always mean it can immediately pay the bills. Sometimes you have to create room for both your dreams and your responsibilities.

That realization isn't failure. It's maturity.

I'M ALLOWED TO FIGURE IT OUT

I don't have to have my entire future planned out today. I can learn, change my mind, try something new, and discover what actually fits the life I'm building.

Maybe the goal isn't choosing between passion and practicality. Maybe it's learning how to build a life where there's room for both.

And that's something I'm still figuring out.
